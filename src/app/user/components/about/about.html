About Us

Transforming Houses Into Dream Homes

Established in 2016, HomeTech Modular & Interiors has become one of Bengaluru's premier interior design firms, specializing in innovative and functional home interior solutions. Our journey began with a vision to provide high-quality, accessible interior design services that transform houses into dream homes.

At HomeTech, we combine expert craftsmanship with a modern approach, offering a comprehensive range of solutions tailored to suit individual lifestyles and preferences. Whether it's a modular kitchen, living room redesign, or complete home makeover, our designs reflect a balance of style and functionality.

Based in Bengaluru, we understand the essence of urban living and create spaces that maximize comfort without compromising on elegance.
